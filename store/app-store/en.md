## Description
A body has been found. Eight suspects, one culprit, and nobody is telling the truth. All you have left is logic.

Pesquisa (Spanish for "investigation") is a deduction game. Every case is a house plan split into rooms, with furniture and a list of clues. Place each suspect: one person per row and per column, never on a table, plant or bookshelf. Once everyone is in place, name the culprit: the only person who was alone with the victim.

PURE LOGIC, NO GUESSING
Every case has exactly one solution, and you can always reach it by reasoning. Cases are generated by the app and each one is checked before you see it.

HINTS THAT EXPLAIN WHY
Stuck? A hint doesn't just hand you the answer: it explains the next deduction and highlights the squares and clues that prove it.

A NEW CASE EVERY DAY
The daily case is the same for everyone. Solve it to keep your streak going, and play any of the last 30 days whenever you like.

FIVE LEVELS
From Apprentice (4×4) to Legend (8×8). For more challenge, try the "No-touch" variant: nobody may stand next to anyone else, not even diagonally.

PLAY AT YOUR OWN PACE
• Guided case: learn the rules in two minutes.
• Pencil notes, crossed-out squares and undo.
• Your game saves itself, clock included.
• Statistics per level: best time, average and hints.
• Share your result without spoiling the solution.
• Light and dark themes, in English, Spanish and Catalan.

NO ADS, NO ACCOUNTS
Pesquisa collects no data and never goes online. Your progress stays on your phone.
